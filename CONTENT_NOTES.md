# MORROW content notes

Snapshot date: 2026-08-17.

## Editorial rules used in this build

1. No invented researchers, experts, studies, statistics or events.
2. Each evergreen article links to the source material used for its factual claims.
3. Medical content stays at public-health/explainer level and does not diagnose or prescribe.
4. Financial content explains principles and risks; it does not provide personalized investment recommendations.
5. Current events use official venue pages and should be refreshed before publication on a later date.
6. When a claim cannot be responsibly supported, the site prefers a transparent limitation to plausible filler.
